Privacy information

Livingworth Academy uses this website and Android app to deliver training and manage learner participation.

Information used by the academy

Depending on the features you use, your account may contain your name, email address, phone number, profile picture, application information, attendance, learning progress, quiz and class activity attempts, assignment submissions, feedback and certificates. Account and delivery records help operate and troubleshoot the service.

Why we use it

We use these records to review applications, provide learning access, track progress, assess work, issue certificates, communicate programme information and support users. Authorised staff access records needed for their role. Leaderboards may show learner names and aggregate results; private activity answers and feedback remain in the relevant signed-in views.

Sign-in and biometrics

Account passwords are stored as hashes. Remember my email saves the email on your device. Optional biometric sign-in protects a saved sign-in key with the phone’s secure storage and biometric system. The academy does not receive your fingerprint images or facial biometric data. Saved biometric sign-ins expire after 30 days and can be revoked under Security.

Phone notifications

Android phone notifications use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. If you enable them, Firebase processes a device registration token to route messages. The academy stores device tokens encrypted and uses generic notification bodies; open the signed-in portal to read private feedback. You can disable phone notifications in the app or Android settings. Availability depends on academy delivery configuration and your phone permission.

Storage and service providers

Learner records and uploaded files are stored in the academy’s backend services. Hosting, email delivery and Google Firebase support relevant service functions. Device storage holds sign-in preferences and app settings. Signed interface updates are downloaded from the academy’s server.

Your choices and requests

Use Security to change your password and manage saved biometric sign-ins. You can update or request corrections to profile information through the portal. Contact academy support through About & Help, or your class mentor, to request access, corrections, account deletion or information about record retention. Some profile changes require administrator approval. Do not send passwords, private keys or other sign-in secrets with a support request.

Keeping information current

Contact the academy if you have questions about how a feature uses your information. This page should be updated when the academy’s data practices change.
