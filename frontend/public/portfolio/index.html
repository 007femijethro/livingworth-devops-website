01 / THE ENGINEER

From deployment
to dependable
operations.

I work where infrastructure, applications and integrations meet.

I studied Computer Engineering at Ekiti State University (EKSU) and have worked as a full-stack developer since 2016. Over the years, my experience in IT has grown across application development, systems integration, infrastructure and production operations. That development background helps me understand how applications are built, deployed and supported throughout their lifecycle.

My work involves deploying services, connecting application environments, troubleshooting production issues and making systems easier to operate. I approach problems by following the evidence: configuration, logs, metrics, network behaviour and the application’s dependencies.

At eTranzact International Plc, I work as a DevOps Engineer in Central & Switching Services, supporting the management and deployment of services. My broader experience spans payment platforms, application integrations, containerised workloads and delivery automation.

Alongside engineering, I lead practical DevOps mentorship at Livingworth Academy. I care about helping people understand the reasoning behind the commands, communicate clearly and build work they can explain.
