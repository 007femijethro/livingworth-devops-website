Terms and Conditions

By applying, you confirm that the information you provide is accurate and that you are applying voluntarily to participate in a Livingworth Academy programme.

Participation

Admission is subject to application review and available spaces. Learners are expected to attend scheduled sessions, participate respectfully, complete assigned work and notify the academy when they cannot attend.

Learning access

Portal access is personal and must not be shared. Course materials may be used for your own learning but must not be copied, sold or redistributed without permission.

Conduct

Livingworth Academy may suspend or remove access for harassment, abuse, dishonesty, persistent disruption or serious failure to follow programme requirements.

Information and privacy

Your application details are used to review your application, manage your learning account, communicate programme information and maintain attendance records.

Changes

Schedules, mentors and programme content may change when reasonably necessary. Important changes will be communicated to enrolled learners.
